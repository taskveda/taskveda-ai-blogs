Who should join this free AI bootcamp?

  • You have used ChatGPT a few times but do not know how to turn it into a skill recruiters care about.

Is the certificate worth anything?

A certificate alone does not get anyone hired, and any bootcamp that promises otherwise is overselling. What makes this one useful is the combination: a certificate plus a public GitHub project plus a portfolio entry plus a resume and LinkedIn review. Recruiters can click your project, read your README and ask you how you built it. That is far stronger than a certificate PDF with nothing behind it.

Treat the certificate as evidence of completion, not as the product. The product is the proof of work.
