How to Get an AI Internship in India as a Fresher (2026)

No IIT tag, no referrals, no experience. A practical system for finding AI internships, proving you can build, and getting interviews from tier-2 and tier-3 colleges.

By Asma Fathima, AI Trainer & Mentor Updated 29 October 2026 11 min read
Quick answer

To get an AI internship for freshers in India, build one documented AI project, rewrite your resume around projects and keywords from the job description, apply to 10 targeted roles a week on Internshala, LinkedIn, Unstop and Wellfound, and send short cold emails with a demo link. Your college name matters less than proof that you can ship something and explain it.

Key takeaways

  • Proof of work beats college pedigree for startup internships.
  • One project with a demo and README is enough to start applying.
  • Tailor every resume and message; volume without relevance is noise.
  • Apply to startups, agencies and small product teams, not just big brands.
  • Never pay a fee for an internship or share sensitive documents early.

What AI internships actually require in 2026

Entry-level AI roles are not all model training. Most internships fall into three buckets:

TypeWhat you doWhat they check
AI tools and automationBuild workflows, prompts and internal automationsPrompting skill, tool fluency, reliability
LLM application developmentBuild small apps with APIs, data and interfacesPython basics, APIs, debugging, GitHub
Data and research supportClean data, evaluate outputs, document findingsAttention to detail, spreadsheets, basic stats

For all three, the interview question that decides your fate is usually the same: "Show me something you built and explain how it works."

The 6-step AI internship plan

Step 1: Pick one role type

Do not apply to everything. Choose AI tools and automation if you are non-coding, or LLM app development if you can write basic Python. A focused resume converts better than a generic one.

Step 2: Build one proof-of-work project

Follow a project like the ones in our AI portfolio projects guide. Publish it on GitHub with screenshots and a short demo video. This single asset will carry your applications.

Step 3: Rewrite your resume around projects

Put projects above coursework. Each bullet should include the problem, the tools and a result. For example: "Built a resume analyzer with Python and Gemini API that flags missing keywords from a job description; documented setup and demo on GitHub."

Step 4: Optimise LinkedIn and GitHub

Your LinkedIn headline should say what you do, not just "student". Pin your best repository, add a profile README and keep your location open to remote roles. Recruiters do check both profiles.

Step 5: Apply in a weekly rhythm

Ten targeted applications per week, each with a tailored resume line and a short note. Track them in a sheet: company, role, date, contact, status, follow-up date.

Step 6: Follow up once, then move on

Send one polite follow-up after five working days. If there is no reply, keep applying. Silence is normal, not personal.

Resume keywords that help you pass filters

Use keywords from the job description only where they are true for you. Common ones for AI internships include:

  • Python, APIs, JSON, Git and GitHub
  • Prompt engineering, LLM, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude
  • Automation, workflow, Make, Zapier
  • Data cleaning, Excel or Google Sheets, visualisation
  • Streamlit, Flask or basic front-end
  • Documentation, testing, debugging

Mirror the exact phrasing from the listing where honest. If the role says "LLM applications", do not write only "AI tools".

Cold email template that gets replies

Subject: Student building AI workflows — quick question Hi [Name], I am a [year] [branch] student at [college]. I built a small AI [project type] that [one concrete outcome]. Demo: [link] I am looking for a 2-3 month AI internship and can contribute [one specific skill, e.g. prompt workflows or Streamlit apps]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call, or should I send my resume? Thank you, [Name] [Portfolio / GitHub link]

Keep it under 120 words, personalise the first line and link one relevant project only. Attach nothing on the first email.

AI internship interview questions to prepare

  • Walk me through your project. What problem did it solve?
  • Why did you choose this tool or model over alternatives?
  • How do you check whether an AI output is correct?
  • What happens when the API fails or returns bad output?
  • How would you improve this project with one more week?
  • Explain prompt engineering to a non-technical teammate.
  • What did you learn from a project that did not work?

Answer with specifics, admit what you do not know, and never claim a skill you cannot demonstrate.

What about stipends and remote internships?

Stipends vary widely by company, city and role, from unpaid or expense-only positions at very early startups to paid internships at funded companies. Remote AI internships are common at startups and agencies. Treat any "unpaid but promising" offer carefully: ask what you will learn, who will mentor you and what deliverables you will own. A good internship gives you either money, mentorship or a strong portfolio piece. Ideally two of the three.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fresher get an AI internship without an IIT tag?

Yes. Startups and small product teams hire on proof of work. A documented project, a tailored resume and a clear explanation matter more than your college name.

Where should students search for AI internships in India?

Internshala, LinkedIn, Unstop, Wellfound and company career pages. Also use alumni networks and startup communities. Apply to 10 targeted roles per week.

What skills do AI internships require in 2026?

Python basics, prompt engineering, LLM APIs, simple data handling and the ability to ship a small project. Non-coding roles value AI tools and automation.

Are work-from-home AI internships available for freshers?

Yes, especially at startups and agencies. Verify the company, ask about mentorship and never pay a fee.

What should I put on my resume for an AI internship?

Projects first, with the problem, tools, a measurable result and a GitHub link. Match job keywords honestly and keep it to one page.

How long does it take to land an AI internship?

With a project, tailored resume and consistent applications, many students get interviews within 4 to 8 weeks. Timelines vary by role and city.
